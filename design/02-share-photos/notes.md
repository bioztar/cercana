# 02 — Share photos (anyone in the family)

Screens: `what-to-share-phone.png` → `pick-photos-phone.png` → `which-event-phone.png` → `caption-and-audience-phone.png`.

## What changed
- "Tell the family" asks what to add: Voice, Photos, or Event.
- After picking photos, the app asks which event they belong to. **Optional**: "No event, just photos" skips it (e.g. a fun selfie).
- Suggestions are existing events close to the photos' date, each with its participants; or "New event" with a title (by voice) and a date pre-filled from the photos.
- Caption is dictated; audience: whole family / Andrey's family / Maxim.

## Open
- No screen yet for the "Voice" option.
- The caption screen always shows the event row; the "no event" variant is not drawn.
