# 05 — Important events: reminders and "Did you go?"

Screens: `andrey-creates-for-mom-phone.png` → `mom-notifications-phone.png` → `mom-did-you-go-phone.png` → `andrey-sees-result-phone.png`.

## What changed
- An event can be marked **Important** and can be for one person only (Andrey creates "Cardiologist appointment" for Mom).
- Important events send reminders to that person's phone at times the creator picks (evening before, morning of).
- After the event a notification asks "Did you go?"; Mom answers Yes / No / Rescheduled, from the notification or in the app, and can add a voice note.
- The creator sees the answer and the delivery status of every reminder.
