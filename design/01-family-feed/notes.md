# 01 — Family feed (Mom's phone)

Screens: `feed-phone.png` (full scroll), `photo-comments-phone.png`, `event-walk-in-the-park-phone.png`, `event-building-the-house-phone.png`.

## What changed
- Every event in the feed shows its participants as initial avatars plus names.
- A photo post that belongs to an event shows an event card under the photos (date, title, participants). Tapping it opens the event page.
- Event page: who was there (editable), all photos of the event with their author, comments, "Add your photos".
- Photo comments screen links back to its event.
- Big "Tell the family" button opens the share flow (02); the Calendar tab opens the calendar (03).
