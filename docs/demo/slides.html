Today

57 million

people are living with dementia worldwide.

Nearly 10 million new cases every year: one every ~3 seconds.

Source: World Health Organization, Dementia fact sheet (2021 figures). who.int

Tomorrow

139 million

by 2050, more than double today.

Alzheimer's disease accounts for 60–70% of cases.

Sources: WHO; Alzheimer's Disease International. who.int · alzint.org

Who carries it

$1.3T

global cost of dementia every year, and about half of it is care given by family and friends

19 bn

hours of unpaid care by nearly 13 million family caregivers, in the US alone, in one year

Sources: WHO (2019 cost); Alzheimer's Association, 2025 Alzheimer's Disease Facts and Figures. alz.org

Why connection matters

45%

of dementia cases could be prevented or delayed.

Social isolation is one of the 14 risk factors we can change. Staying close to family isn't just kind. It's care.

Source: Livingston et al., Dementia prevention, intervention, and care: 2024 report of the Lancet standing Commission. thelancet.com

What Cercana does

  1. She never has to remember. The phone tells her her day and who everyone is.
  2. She's not just a viewer. She answers her family by voice.
  3. The family knows she's OK. "Did you go to the doctor?" comes back to them.
