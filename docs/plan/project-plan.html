Cercana — plan to a demoable MVP

Status as of Thursday 24 September 2026, 13:40 · Live: https://cercana.pro7ocol.com · Repo: github.com/bioztar/cercana

What "demo-ready" means Vitaly can pick up his iPhone with Cercana installed, put a laptop next to it, and in three minutes show:
  1. The phone speaks the morning briefing: "Good morning Maria… Anna is in London until Sunday. Tomorrow is Lucia's birthday."
  2. Tap Anna's face → WhatsApp call.
  3. On the laptop, a family member posts a voice note with a photo → it appears in "From your family" on the phone.
  4. The family member taps Ping → the iPhone notifies, then says aloud "Anna says: thinking of you".
  5. A trip added to Anna's Google/iCloud calendar shows up on her card.
  6. A new relative opens the invite link, picks "I'm Pedro", and is in.
Web does all of this today except the native push in step 4. The iPhone app is the main gap.

Honest limits of the demo

Say these out loud if asked
  • No real login yet. Anyone with the invite code can join; roles are enforced in the app only. It's demo-grade. Real sign-in comes before any stranger uses it.
  • The phone can't talk from the lock screen. iOS shows the notification; the voice plays once it's tapped open. Every app has this limit.
  • No call recording, no always-on microphone. iOS doesn't allow either; family-written moments and one-tap voice notes replace them.
  • Google calendar changes can take hours to show up. Google publishes its own feed slowly. iCloud updates faster.
  • Web gets no push when the tab is closed. Push notifications work in the iPhone app; on web they need the page open.
